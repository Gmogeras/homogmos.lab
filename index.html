Exploring Genomes.
Finding Life.

An independent laboratory exploring the hidden history of life preserved within genomes.

Homogmos Lab combines evolutionary genomics, computational biology, paleogenomics, scientific software and open exploration to ask better questions about how life records its own history.

read_manifesto()open_projects/

THE QUESTION

How much of life's history is still hidden inside genomes?

Every project in Homogmos Lab starts somewhere around this question. A genome is not only sequence. It is an archive shaped by ancestry, recombination, selection, drift, migration, extinction and time.

$ ls research/

Different research lines, one shared goal: learning how to read biological history from genomic data.

R01

Evolutionary Genomics

Selection, drift, demography, recombination and divergence across genomes.

 R02

Genomic Architecture

Local genealogies, TMRCA and the coexistence of different evolutionary histories.

 R03

Paleogenomics

Using ancient and modern data to reconstruct populations and biological change through time.
