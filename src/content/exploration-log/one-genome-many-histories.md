---
title: "One genome does not have only one history"
description: "Why local genealogies change the way we tell evolutionary stories."
date: 2026-08-09
status: note
tags: [local-genealogies, recombination, evolution]
---
A single species tree is useful, but recombination makes genomic ancestry local. Different regions of the same genome can point toward different pasts without implying that evolution is incoherent.

This long-form note will develop the relationship between recombination, local trees, TMRCA, incomplete lineage sorting, selection and the distinction between a species history and the history of a genomic segment.
