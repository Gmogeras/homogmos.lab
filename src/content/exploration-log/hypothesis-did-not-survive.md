description: "A negative functional-enrichment result redirected the mitonuclear project toward a broader genomic question."
date: 2026-08-07

tags: [mitonuclear, TMRCA, negative-results]
---
We asked whether nuclear regions with mitochondrial-like TMRCA would be enriched for mitochondrial functions. The first functional analyses did not support that simple model.

That negative result redirected the project toward a broader question: **why do some nuclear regions share similar coalescent times at all?**

The current work is therefore moving away from a binary mitochondrial-versus-nuclear comparison and toward genomic heterogeneity, local genealogies and recurrent TMRCA regimes.
