---
title: "BIOATLAS becomes a platform, not a map"
description: "The project expanded from geographic visualization into a broader interface for biodiversity and evolution."
date: 2026-08-09
status: working
tags: [BIOATLAS, biodiversity, visualization]
---
BIOATLAS began as a geographic visualization idea. It is now being designed as a broader interface connecting species, phylogeny, deep time, traits and natural-history storytelling.

The objective is not to build another static encyclopedia. It is to let visitors navigate biological context.
