Working analyses of *Steinernema carpocapsae* are being reorganized around three TMRCA regimes—recent, intermediate and deep—rather than one binary comparison with the mitochondrial genome.

The next goal is to test whether these regimes form meaningful genomic blocks and whether their distribution across chromosomes contains biological structure beyond the windowing procedure itself.
